# Course Plan — Living Curriculum (12 Weeks / 84 Days)

> **Roadmap only.** Detailed lessons exist for Days 1–3. Days 4–84 are planned stubs until you reach them. 
> **Last updated:** 2026-08-21

---

## Curriculum Philosophy

- Engineering understanding over mathematics
- Architecture before vendor technology
- Enterprise examples throughout
- Flexible — modify based on gaps, evaluation results, and new technology
- Do not mark complete without demonstrated understanding

## Daily Lesson Format

Every **activated** lesson includes:

Learning Objectives · Why This Matters · Prerequisites · Core Concepts · Mental Model · Architecture Diagram · Engineering Perspective · Tradeoffs · Common Failure Modes · Production/Security/Observability/Cost Considerations · Hands-On Exercise · Design Exercise · Practice Questions · Self Assessment · Key Takeaways · Summary
